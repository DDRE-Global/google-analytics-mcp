Document workflow is the strongest conversion path

42% of active users who view the document list go on to send a document (67 → 28 users). This is the most reliable end-to-end product outcome in June.

AML create funnel loses ~92% before status review

Only 3 of 38 AML list viewers reach status review (8% end-to-end). 53% of list viewers never start creation; 56% of starters don't submit.

Login is the dominant front door

39% of sessions (331 of 855) land on /login. 66% of active users who view login also reach home in the auth funnel (93 of 141). Login remains the highest-traffic page by page views (2,206).

Cross-feature navigation is common

Users frequently pivot between AML and Documents mid-journey. After viewing AML list, 14 active users next visit Documents — suggesting workflow fragmentation or multi-tasking agents.

Strategic Recommendations

Data-backed priorities from funnel (active users) and navigation (page view) analysis.

1. Fix AML list → create conversion

53% of AML list viewers abandon before starting creation. 14 pivot to Documents instead. Audit list UX: CTA visibility, empty states, and loading friction.

2. Reduce send-page abandonment

39% drop off at document send (18 of 46 active users). 20 show "no next action" — likely form errors or incomplete sends. Instrument wizard step events (gap noted in tracking spec).

3. Consolidate post-auth routing

Login → Home is strong (168 page views) but login remains 42% of all page views. Consider default deep-links to last-used feature (Documents or AML) for returning agents.

4. Double down on Document workflow

42% list-to-sent conversion is the benchmark. Use this flow's patterns (clear steps, strong list→action path) as template for AML wizard redesign.

5. Investigate 29 Jun traffic spike

3,100 events on a single day (4× the daily average) with only 36 sessions suggests automated activity, bulk imports, or a tracking anomaly — validate with engineering before drawing user-behaviour conclusions for that date.
