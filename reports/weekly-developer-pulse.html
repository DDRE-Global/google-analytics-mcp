Insight 1

Weekly active advisors on production: 53 (−20% vs 66 prior). That sits in line with our known base (~59 Strapi users / 73 agents) — adoption is real but still early, and WoW softness is worth watching.

Insight 2

Usage concentrates on home, login, documents, and AML. Monday reached only 9 users. Login drew 45 of 53 actives (136 views) — worth checking whether session expiry or re-auth friction is inflating that load.

Insight 3

Biggest conversion leak is document send: 18 entered the flow, only 5 completed (28%). AML holds better once started (14 create → 8 submit, 57%), but a third of list viewers never create. Priority tests: send-step friction (recipients, validation, MoS gaps) and AML list→create / mid-wizard drop-off.
